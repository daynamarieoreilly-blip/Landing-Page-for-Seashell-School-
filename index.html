Behind the build 🌊

Notes and claim-checking for the fictional Seashell Schools and Activities student landing page, created for an assessment on 19 October 2026.

Who this is for

Independent surf and watersport schools along Ireland's Wild Atlantic Way that run scheduled group lessons affected by weather. This is a chosen marketing niche, not a claim that real schools use the product.

What the linked alpha actually does

  • Shows four days of lesson sessions with names, group sizes and contact details.
  • Offers a large Rebook customer action and a separate Cancel entire session action.
  • Moves affected bookings to a saved follow-up list.
  • Shows up to three upcoming sessions with sufficient free places for the group.
  • Marks bookings waiting 48 hours or longer red.
  • Shows booking count, people waiting and potential revenue at risk based on fictional lesson pricing.
  • Opens a prepared customer email in the device's email application; a staff member must review and send it.
  • Uses browser localStorage: changes remain on that browser after refresh, but do not sync across devices.

Assumptions, invented copy and planned features

  • The customer quotation on the landing page is invented, not a real testimonial.
  • €99 one-time setup and €150 per six-month season are proposed pricing assumptions, not verified market rates or a live sales offer.
  • A real commercial service, production support, multi-device synchronisation and automatic message delivery are not included in the current alpha and would require future development.
  • The interest form is a visual demonstration only: JavaScript prevents submission and shows a thank-you message. It does not send or store personal data.
  • The demo uses fictional customer records and fictional sample data. No real customer numbers, ratings, testimonials or sales results are claimed.

Visual sources and credits

The animated wave artwork and decorative elements were created for this page using original CSS and inline SVG; no externally sourced photographs, stock imagery, real business logos or third-party brand assets are used. Surfboard, shamrock and shell emoji are rendered by the visitor's device/system emoji font, so their appearance varies by platform.

Design decisions

Phone-first layout, strong contrast, generous touch targets, one main sales action (interest form), and a separate Try the demo link. Motion is decorative and disabled automatically when the device requests reduced motion.
